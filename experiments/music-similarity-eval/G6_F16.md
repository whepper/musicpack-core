# G-6 — f16 versus f32 ranking equivalence

> **Status: EXPERIMENT. Not a decision.** This document records the methodology
> and the acceptance criteria **before** any result was produced, then records
> what the experiment found. G-6 remains **OPEN** whatever the outcome: a
> satisfactory result supplies evidence for a later explicit human sign-off, and
> nothing here closes the gate.
>
> Scope: a numerical experiment inside `experiments/music-similarity-eval/`. No
> production code, no `.mpack` format change, no ADR change, no model download,
> no perceptual evaluation. The reference storage encoding stays `f32le`.

---

## 1. The question

Does storing a similarity vector as IEEE-754 binary16 instead of the reference
IEEE-754 binary32 change similarity rankings beyond an explicitly predeclared
tolerance?

This is a question about **numerical representation**, not about music. It
cannot establish that f16 is perceptually equivalent to f32; that is G-7, and
G-7 is untouched by this work. The strongest statement this experiment can
support is of the form:

> For the representation and corpus actually tested, f16 quantization preserves
> the specified numerical similarity rankings within the predeclared tolerance.

and no stronger.

## 2. Predeclared acceptance criteria

### 2.1 Provenance of these thresholds

These thresholds are **not new**. They were written into
[`FORMAT_SPEC.md`](FORMAT_SPEC.md) §7.3 *before* this experiment existed, as
proposed G-6 criteria, on the explicit principle that *"a threshold chosen after
seeing the numbers is not a gate"*. They are reproduced here verbatim and are
used exactly as written. No threshold below was chosen, adjusted or relaxed after
any result was inspected.

| # | Criterion | Threshold | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | `max abs(Δcos)` over every evaluated pair | ≤ 1e-3 | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |
| C2 | Minimum cosine observed in the f16 world, every pair | ≥ 0.99 | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |
| C3 | Spearman ρ, full pairwise rank order | ≥ 0.999 | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |
| C4 | Mean top-10 Jaccard over the seeds | ≥ 0.98 | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |
| C5 | Top-1 unchanged | ≥ 90 % of seeds | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |
| C6 | Relative-order swaps | ≤ 0.1 % of all pairs | FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 |

### 2.2 Supplementary criteria, also predeclared

FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 predates this experiment and does not cover every metric the
experiment brief requires. The following were added **before any run**, and are
binding in the same way. They were derived from the format's intended semantics:
a ranking is "preserved" if the neighbour set barely moves and the moved members
barely move within it.

| # | Criterion | Threshold | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C7 | Mean top-5 Jaccard over the seeds | ≥ 0.97 | C4 fixes top-10; top-5 is the tighter neighbourhood a listener would perceive, and is expected to be *more* stable than top-10, so a slightly lower bound is the conservative direction |
| C8 | Mean top-1 unchanged over the seeds | ≥ 0.90 | Identical to C5, restated as a mean so the two corpora are directly comparable |
| C9 | Maximum rank displacement, any neighbour in any seed's top-10 | ≤ 10 positions | A displacement beyond a top-10-sized window means the neighbourhood was replaced, not reordered |
| C10 | Mean absolute cosine difference over all pairs | ≤ 1e-4 | One order of magnitude tighter than C1: C1 bounds the worst case, this bounds the typical case, so a distribution that is mostly fine cannot hide behind one outlier |

### 2.3 What "relative-order swap" means

A pair `(a, b)` with `a ≠ b` is a **relative-order swap** when
`sign(cos_f32(q,a) − cos_f32(q,b)) ≠ sign(cos_f16(q,a) − cos_f16(q,b))` for some
query `q`, i.e. f16 reverses which of the two the query prefers. Swaps are
counted per unordered pair, once, if any query reverses them. This is the
metric that most directly measures ranking damage, and it is C6.

### 2.4 Verdict rule, fixed in advance

- **PASS** — C1…C10 all satisfied **and** the corpus limitation in §3 is
  resolved.
- **FAIL** — any of C1…C10 violated.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** — the experiment cannot establish the criteria, including the
  case where the criteria are satisfied on a substitute corpus while the corpus
  the criteria were written against is unavailable.

The verdict rule is not renegotiable after results are seen.

## 3. Scope limitation, recorded before the run

**The recorded Discogs-EffNet run outputs are not present in this repository, so
C1…C6 cannot be evaluated on the corpus they were written against.**

FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 specifies the measurement over "the recorded runs": 45 tracks /
15 albums, two model variants, per-track f32 vectors, at two patch hops. Those
vectors were written by the original spike to external output directories. They
are not committed here and are not on disk:

- the experiment `.gitignore` excludes `*.json`, and `embeddings.json` /
  `neighbors.json` live there;
- the model artefact itself is never downloaded into the repository
  (CC BY-NC-SA, deliberately not redistributed);
- no committed fixture contains embedding vectors.

This is a **data-availability** limitation, not a methodological one, and it is
not a finding against f16. It is recorded here, before running anything, so that
the verdict cannot later be presented as a corpus result.

What follows therefore evaluates C1…C10 on a **deterministic surrogate corpus**
(§4) that reproduces the *shape* of the recorded run — 45 tracks, 15 clusters of
3, 1280 dimensions, unit vectors — and on an adversarial suite (§6). Because
C1…C6 are corpus-conditional as written, satisfying them on a surrogate does not
satisfy them as specified. This is why the verdict in §9 is expected to be
INCONCLUSIVE even if every number is comfortable, and why §10 states the exact
mechanical step that would resolve it.

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Representations compared

Exactly the comparison the brief requires, and nothing else:

```text
reference : v            (canonical f32, L2-normalized by the producing pipeline)
candidate : f16_to_f32(f16_encode(v))
```

There is no second vector-generation path, no re-encoding of audio, and no
alternative model. `f16_encode` is `docfmt::f32_to_f16_bits` — the RNE
implementation already specified in FORMAT_SPEC §7.4 and already pinned by
`binary16_bit_patterns_are_the_standard_ones` and
`binary16_rounding_is_to_nearest_with_ties_to_even`. `f16_to_f32` is
`docfmt::f16_bits_to_f32`.

### 4.2 No extra normalization

`f16_to_f32` output is **not** re-normalized. The producing pipeline already
L2-normalizes before storage (`eval::pool_mean_norm`: per-window L2, mean, track
L2), and the comparison procedure `eval::cosine` divides by both norms, so it is
normalization-robust by construction. Adding a renormalization step would hide
exactly the norm perturbation f16 introduces, which is one of the things under
test. The norm perturbation is measured and reported instead (§5.5).

### 4.3 Established comparison and ranking procedures, reused unchanged

| Step | Function reused |
| --- | --- |
| Cosine | `eval::cosine` — f64 accumulation over f32 inputs, returns `f32` |
| Ranking | `eval::nearest` — excludes the query, sorts by score descending with `total_cmp`, tie-breaks by index, truncates to `top_k` |
| Album aggregate | `eval::aggregate_albums` — mean over member vectors, then L2 |

`eval::nearest` is used as-is. It is the ranking procedure the experiment already
measured patch-hop stability with, so the f16 comparison is against the same
yardstick as the recorded evidence.

### 4.4 Corpus generation

Deterministic by construction, with no new dependency and no floating-point
randomness in the generator: a SHA-256 counter-mode stream supplies the values,
so the corpus is byte-reproducible on any platform and any toolchain.

- **15 cluster centres**, 1280-D unit vectors, cross-cluster cosine drawn to a
  controlled band so that rankings are not trivially stable.
- **3 tracks per cluster**, `normalize(centre + noise · s)`, giving a within-album
  cosine distribution comparable to what the experiment reported for same-album
  neighbours.
- **1280 dimensions**, matching Discogs-EffNet `multi`.
- Track identities are anonymous and positional (`t00`…`t44`, cluster `c00`…`c14`).
  No artist, album, title, path or filename is generated or stored.

The same generator is run at **512** and **16** dimensions to quantify how the
quantization error scales with dimension, since that scaling is the substantive
numerical question and §7's thresholds are dimension-independent.

### 4.5 Metrics

Per seed, self excluded: top-1 / top-5 / top-10 exact set overlap (Jaccard) and
rank displacement; count of seeds whose top-1, top-5 or top-10 set changes;
maximum and mean rank displacement; the percentile distribution of displacement.
Over all pairs: `Δcos` minimum, mean absolute, maximum absolute, and relative
difference **only where it is mathematically meaningful** — relative difference
on a cosine that crosses zero is meaningless and is reported as such rather than
averaged. Plus Spearman ρ over the full pairwise order and the relative-order swap
count of §2.3.

## 6. Adversarial and boundary cases

Constructed deterministically, and reported **separately** from the surrogate
corpus. The purpose is to discover whether the representation has a known
numerical failure mode, not to manufacture a production failure.

| Case | What it stresses |
| --- | --- |
| near-identical | Two vectors differing in the last f32 bit |
| tiny separation | Cosine separation swept from 1e-1 down to 1e-7 |
| near ties | Three or more candidates within a quantization-sized band |
| full f16 range | Components spanning the binary16 normal range |
| subnormal boundary | Values at, just above and just below 2^-24 |
| rounding-sensitive | Components that land exactly on a tie, forcing RNE-even |
| minimal dimension | d = 2 and d = 3, where per-component relative error is largest |
| all-equal components | A vector with no small components to average error away |

The album aggregate is unaffected to f32 print precision: it is a mean over three
member vectors, and the per-member errors are independent enough to cancel.

### 7.4 Resolution limit — the substantive finding

The `|Δcos|` sweep is the wrong thing to look at, and the first run of this
experiment demonstrated why: below about 1e-4 the perturbation goes to **zero**,
because two vectors that close quantize to the *same* binary16 vector. "No error"
there means "the two have become indistinguishable".

The informative measurement is therefore the closest pair f16 still
distinguishes, sweeping a target cosine directly:

| Dimension | closest distinguishable cosine | 1 − cosine | vs f32's own limit near 1.0 |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 4 | 0.999999940395 | 5.96e-8 | at f32's limit |
| 16 | 0.999999999970 | 2.98e-11 | at f32's limit |
| 64 | 0.999999999970 | 2.98e-11 | at f32's limit |
| 512 | 0.999999999970 | 2.98e-11 | at f32's limit |
| 1280 | 0.999999999970 | 2.98e-11 | at f32's limit |
| 2048 | 0.999999999970 | 2.98e-11 | at f32's limit |

**At every dimension from 16 upward, f32 is the binding resolution limit, not
f16.** binary16 can resolve pairs that binary32 can no longer tell apart, because
the cosine is accumulated in f64 from the quantized components while the f32
cosine itself is stored with only 24 significand bits. Only at d=4 does f16
become limiting, and only barely.

This matters for how the result should be read: for a 1280-dimensional embedding,
f16 is not the precision bottleneck anywhere near the range a similarity index
operates in.

## 8. Results — adversarial and boundary suite

Reported separately from the corpus, as intended. Constructed cases, not corpus

| Case | dim | max abs(Δcos) | swaps | new ties | overflow | reading |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| near-identical | 8 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | one f32 ULP apart is below binary16 resolution: the two become the same vector |
| near-tie | 6 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | three candidates inside a quantization-sized band: no reversal, because the band is narrower than one binary16 step |
| full-f16-range | 8 | 9.537e-7 | 0 | 0 | no | components across the whole binary16 normal range are fine |
| **subnormal-boundary** | 4 | **7.954e-2** | 0 | 0 | no | **the one genuine failure mode — see below** |
| rounding-tie | 4 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | values exactly on a binary16 tie round to even, identically in both directions |
| dimension-2 | 2 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | no movement at all |
| dimension-3 | 3 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | no movement at all |
| all-equal-components | 4 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | no | no movement |

### 8.1 The subnormal failure mode, quantified

The `subnormal-boundary` case moves the cosine by **0.0795** — four orders of
magnitude worse than anything else measured. The cause is structural, not
incidental: below `2^-14` a binary16 value is a subnormal, quantized to integer
multiples of `2^-24`, so relative precision degrades as the value shrinks. The
`magnitude-precision` sweep measures the knee directly:

| Component magnitude | f16 relative error |
| --- | ---: |
| ≥ 1.3e-2 (the whole normal range) | 1.149e-4 (constant) |
| 1.0e-4 | 1.149e-4 |
| 1.6e-5 | 1.838e-3 |
| 2.6e-5 | 4.596e-4 |
| 1.0e-6 | 7.353e-3 |
| 5.1e-7 | 1.765e-1 |
| 2.5e-8 | 1.000e0 (rounds to zero) |

**Why this does not threaten the format as specified.** A unit-normalized
1280-dimensional vector has components around `1/sqrt(1280) ≈ 0.028`, three
orders of magnitude above the knee, where relative error is the constant
1.149e-4. The failure requires a vector whose components are themselves near or
below `2^-14` — which no L2-normalized embedding of dimension ≫ 4 can be.

**But it is a real constraint on future profiles, and it is the one finding from
this experiment that should constrain a decision.** The `f16le` encoding is only
safe for vectors whose components sit in the binary16 normal range. A profile
that emitted small-magnitude components, or one that did not L2-normalize, would
be silently and severely damaged. That is a profile requirement, and it belongs
in the profile registry alongside the other fingerprint fields, not in the
container format.

## 9. Verdict

### 9.1 Classification

**FAIL** — under the rule fixed in §2.4 before the run.

C2 and C6 are violated. C1, C3, C4, C5, C7, C8, C9 and C10 are satisfied with
large margins.

### 9.2 Why the two failures are not evidence that f16 is unsuitable

Stating this precisely, because a bare "FAIL" would be misleading in the other
direction.

**C2 fails for a reason that has nothing to do with f16.** The criterion demands
that *every* pair in the corpus have cosine ≥ 0.99. The **reference f32 world
violates it identically**: its minimum cosine is −0.076396, against the f16
world's −0.076398. A criterion that the unmodified representation also fails is
measuring the corpus, not the encoding. No realistic 45-track corpus spanning 15
albums has every pair above 0.99; cross-album pairs do not. The parenthetical
intent recorded with C2 — *"quantization must not push distinct tracks into
'unrelated' or create false near-duplicates"* — is about the **change** caused by
quantization, and on that reading the measurement is: quantization created **zero**
false near-duplicates (no pair moved toward cosine 1.0) and moved the minimum
cosine by 2.1e-6.

**C6 fails on reversals that are not reversals of anything.** The three swaps at
1280-D crossed reference cosine margins of **1.19e-6, 1.43e-6 and 2.49e-6** —
between one and two parts per million. The f16 noise floor on this corpus is
`max abs(Δcos) = 1.835e-5`, roughly an order of magnitude larger. Every reversal
therefore happened between two candidates that the reference representation
itself ordered arbitrarily, deep in the tail: the top-10 sets are identical for
all 45 seeds and the maximum rank displacement is **zero**. A criterion that
counts an arbitrary ordering among near-zero-cosine tail candidates as "ranking
damage" will be violated by any representation whose noise is non-zero, including
a change of `f32` accumulation order.

Both failures are **threshold-formulation** problems, and both would be resolved
by a criterion expressed relative to the corpus and to the noise floor — for
example a swap budget restricted to pairs whose reference margin exceeds
`max abs(Δcos)`, and a C2 restated as a bound on quantization-induced movement
rather than on absolute cosine level.

### 9.3 What this verdict does not mean

It does **not** mean f16 is perceptually equivalent to f32. That question
belonged to G-7, which is untouched by this work and is closed as a technical
release gate (ADR 0017 §10.5).

It does **not** mean f16 is safe for every future profile. §8.1 is a concrete
counterexample for any vector with subnormal-scale components.

It does **not** close G-6. Per §11 it supplies evidence for a human decision.

And it is a verdict on a **surrogate** corpus, because the recorded corpus could
not be read (§3). C2 in particular is entirely corpus-dependent, and C6 depends
on how many near-tied pairs a corpus contains, so neither can be settled on a
surrogate. A human reviewer may reasonably treat this as *"the criteria need
re-specification, and the measurement must then be repeated on the recorded
corpus"* rather than as *"f16 is bad"*. Both readings keep `f32le` as the
reference encoding in the meantime, which is where the format already stands.

### 9.4 The measurement instrument was corrected mid-experiment

Recorded for audit. The first execution of this experiment produced two metrics
that turned out to be uninformative, and a third that was wrong:

1. a `toolchain=rustc 0.1.0` provenance line that reported the **crate** version
   as the toolchain — removed, and the real toolchain recorded in `RUN.txt`;
2. a `|Δcos|`-versus-separation sweep that is uninformative in the interesting
   direction (§7.4) — kept, but supplemented by the resolution limit;
3. a resolution probe using a cos-wave construction whose "separation" parameter
   did not produce a controlled vector distance, giving non-monotonic nonsense
   (an empty `RangeInclusive` and a reversed multiplier order were both bugs
   here) — replaced with a construction that targets the cosine directly.

**No threshold and no verdict rule was changed at any point.** The corrections
were to the instrument, and each is described where it appears. The criteria in
§2 are the criteria the verdict in §9 was computed against.

## 10. What would close G-6

The measurement FORMAT_SPEC §7.3 describes is a post-processing step over
already-recorded vectors: no model, no audio, no inference. Given the output
directory of a recorded run, the confirmation is mechanical:

1. read each track's f32 vector from the run's `embeddings.json`;
2. `f16_encode` → `f16_to_f32` each component;
3. rank with `eval::nearest` in both worlds and compute C1…C10;
4. record the profile fingerprint, model variant, patch hop and corpus identity
   alongside the result.

Until that is run against a recorded corpus, C1…C6 remain unverified **as
written**, and this experiment supplies evidence rather than a verdict.

## 11. Reproducibility and privacy record

`fixtures/g6/RUN.txt` records the identity of the run: toolchain, the exact
representations compared, the conversion semantics, the vector source and why a
surrogate was used, the corpus and candidate counts, the determinism method, and
the privacy position. `fixtures/g6/REPORT.txt` is the regenerating artefact.

`REPORT.txt` deliberately contains **no** timestamp, path, host name or toolchain
string, so that two runs are byte-comparable. Verified: three independent
executions produced the identical digest

```text
921e58f1c9e3ad4127ce87c0…  REPORT.txt
```

and `g6_tests` enforces both the run-to-run identity and the agreement between
the committed artefact and the current code.

No artefact contains a username, a local filesystem path, a music-library path,
an artist, album or track name, or any secret. Track identities are anonymous and
positional (`t00`…`t44`, clusters `c00`…`c14`). Enforced by
`g6_tests::no_result_artefact_leaks_a_path_a_username_or_metadata`, and
`g6_tests::the_experiment_claims_nothing_about_perceptual_quality` fails if the
report's own vocabulary ever drifts into a perceptual claim.

## 12. Storage comparison — contextual only

Never a criterion, and it did not influence any threshold or the verdict.

| Dimension | f32 bytes/vector | f16 bytes/vector | Reduction |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 512 | 2 048 | 1 024 | 50.0 % |
| 1280 | 5 120 | 2 560 | 50.0 % |
| 2048 | 8 192 | 4 096 | 50.0 % |
| 4096 | 16 384 | 8 192 | 50.0 % |

The reduction is exactly 50 % at every dimension, because binary16 has half the
significand-plus-exponent width of binary32. There is no dimension at which f16
is *more* economical relative to f32; the only thing dimension changes is the
absolute size.

## 13. What this experiment is not

- It is not a perceptual evaluation. G-7 owned that question; it is closed as a
  technical release gate (ADR 0017 §10.5).
- It is not a claim that f16 is safe for every profile. §8.1 is a concrete
  counterexample.
- It is not a measurement on the recorded Discogs-EffNet corpus, which could not
  be read (§3).
- It is not a decision. The reference encoding stays `f32le`; the format
  specification, ADR 0017 and the reference codec are unchanged by this work.
